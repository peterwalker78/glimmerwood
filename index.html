The wisp is dozing

It can’t see anything from here — no history, nothing to watch. This is only a page. But it left the light on, and somewhere to go.

Good places

    Chosen for the hour. They change every couple of hours.

    Worth knowing

    What a phone is good at

    Calls. Messages. Maps when you’re lost. A camera, a torch, tickets, music on a walk.

    Those are worth carrying. Most of the rest of the internet is better at a desk — somewhere you sit down with it, and then stand up and leave.

    So if you’d like to try something: take the apps that shout off this screen, and leave this here instead. Nothing bad happens if you put them back.

    Put it where an app was

    iPhone. Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Android. The menu, then Add to Home screen.

    It opens on its own after that, without a browser around it.

    Where the wisp actually lives

    Glimmerwood is a browser for Linux with the wisp in it. It watches how your time online is going — restoring, ordinary, wearing — and shows it by how it looks, without ever saying a word about it. The garden at the top of this page is the one it keeps for you.

    Download the latest release
