      The wisp

      After a little while on a site that's on none of the lists. At most three questions a day, never at night, and never while you're typing.

      The night

      At night the wisp winds down with you: late scrolling weighs a little more, and restful pages rest a little less. Set it to when you'd like to be heading to sleep.

      Good news in your terminal

      Newsboat reads feeds in a terminal: words only, and when you've read what's new, it's done. Glimmerwood can give it these. Most report what's working on real problems; the light ones are simply cheerful.

      Newsboat hasn't been run on this computer yet. Install it from your distribution, run newsboat once in a terminal so it makes its folder, then come back here.
